Who I am

I'm currently working through a Bachelor of Science in Information Systems at CSB Antipolo, where the trimestral calendar keeps a steady mix of programming, business analysis, and general education coursework on my desk at any given time. I'm early in the technical side of things — still building fluency in Java fundamentals — but the business-analysis and financial-reporting side of my coursework has already had me producing real deliverables: SWOT recommendations, ratio analysis, and research writing under APA.

The thread running through most of what I do is a preference for empirical results over theoretical speculation — I'd rather test something and see what happens than argue about it in the abstract. That, and a personal philosophy that kindness only means something when it's freely chosen rather than demanded.
